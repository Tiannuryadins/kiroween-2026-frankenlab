# Product — FrankenLab

FrankenLab is a Kiroween 2026 hackathon entry (Frankenstein category): a monster
laboratory where users stitch a monster from classic monster parts and bring it to
life with lightning.

Audience: hackathon judges and Halloween fans opening a link — it must work instantly
with no login, no install, no payment, no network beyond the static files.

Product rules:
- Everything runs client-side. No backend, no accounts, no tracking.
- The app must be fun within 10 seconds of loading: a monster is already on the slab.
- Every feature must work offline from a static host (GitHub Pages).
- Halloween tone: playful-spooky, never gory. All art is original SVG drawn in code.
